## Amendment 5 — Marketing source connection and reporting

**Ruling: ArkFlow connects advertising lead-source data from Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads and TikTok into the customer journey, on all three levels.**

### What this supersedes

The C-13 ruling of 15 August 2026 stated: *"Do not claim that Google, Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn are all currently integrated."* That ruling was made when no source document established which integrations ArkFlow could configure. The founder has since confirmed the capability.

**LinkedIn is not included** in this amendment. It was named in the original C-13 ruling but not in the approval, and it is not claimed anywhere.

### The claim that may be made

ArkFlow **connects ad-platform lead-source data into the same customer record**, so a business can follow a channel through to the enquiry, the booking and the revenue in one place rather than four.

### The claim that may NOT be made

- That ArkFlow replaces or manages the native advertising platforms. It does not. Spec §07 is unchanged: **ArkFlow does not run paid advertising and does not generate demand.**
- Any figure, conversion rate, attribution accuracy or performance result. ArkFlow has no client data.
- That every platform exposes the same depth of data. They do not, and copy must say so.

### Required qualifier

Public copy carries, in substance: *what each platform reports back varies, and ArkFlow connects what they expose rather than replacing the native ad managers.* This is not hedging — it is true of every attribution product, and stating it is what makes the rest of the claim credible.

## Amendment 6 — Past-customer reactivation moves to Operate

**Ruling: RenewalRadar is included in Operate and Scale. It is no longer Scale-only.**

### v1.0 text superseded

| Location | v1.0 says | Status |
|---|---|---|
| §04 Operate — Limitations | *"No past-customer reactivation engine — available in Scale."* | ❌ Superseded |
| §05 Scale — "What Scale adds beyond Operate" | Lists past-customer reactivation among Scale's additions | ⚠️ Amended — reactivation removed from the list; website and Voice AI remain |
| §10 Package Matrix | Past-customer reactivation: Operate **"—"** | ❌ Becomes **"Included"** |

### Corrected position

| Level | RenewalRadar |
|---|---|
| Respond | Not included |
| **Operate** | **Included** |
| **Scale** | **Included** |

### Consequence recorded

Amendment 1 moved the AI Voice Agent **up** to Scale-only, on the reasoning that selling a Scale differentiator as an Operate add-on erodes the tier ladder. This amendment moves a different Scale differentiator **down**.

Both were founder decisions and both stand. But the combined effect should be stated plainly: **Scale is now differentiated by the professional website and the AI Voice Agent, plus the monthly strategy call.** Reactivation is no longer part of what separates it. Anyone writing Scale positioning should work from that, not from §05 as originally written.

## Amendment 7 — Personalised recall (new deliverable, all levels)

**Ruling: a recall workflow that sends personalised messages on a customer's birthday or after a defined interval since their last visit is included on all three levels.**

### What it is

An automation that watches two things — the calendar date and how long it has been since a customer last came in — and sends a personalised message when either rule fires. The business writes the message and sets the interval. If the business wants to attach an offer, it decides what that offer is.

### How it differs from RenewalRadar

These sit next to each other and the distinction must be kept clear in copy, or a prospect will reasonably ask what they are paying extra for:

| | Personalised recall | RenewalRadar |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | **A rule the business sets** — a date, or an interval since the last visit | **Engagement signals** — the system identifies who is going quiet or at risk |
| Question it answers | "Who is due?" | "Who is slipping away?" |
| Levels | All three | Operate and Scale |

### Constraints

- Messages are written and approved by the business. ArkFlow does not compose clinical content, make treatment recommendations or invent offers.
- Any discount is the client's own commercial decision. Spec §09's never-discount rule governs **ArkFlow's own fees** and is untouched by this.
- No claimed uplift, response rate or revenue figure.

### Naming

This deliverable has **no canonical product name yet**. BookingBot, InvoiceFlow, RenewalRadar and AI Voice Agent are the existing named systems. Public copy currently describes it functionally. If it should carry a name in the ARK- product family, that is a founder decision.
