# Content plan — ten Singapore long-tail posts

Drafted 21 October 2026. Outlines only. **Nothing here may be
published with invented data**: no made-up percentages, no survey we
did not run, no client results, no competitor claims. Where an outline
says "cite", it means find and read the primary source first — the
PDPC advisory, the MOH guidance, the Meta policy page — and link it.

House rules carried over from the published articles:

- One primary long-tail keyword each, from
  [keyword-map.md](./keyword-map.md). None competes with a service page.
- Every post links to its service page with descriptive anchor text,
  and to one or two sibling posts.
- Singapore specifics or it does not ship: SGT, PDPA, PayNow, MOH,
  public-holiday weekends, the actual price bands clinics quote.
- No outcome promises. The response-time claim is "in minutes".

---

### 1. PDPA and WhatsApp marketing: what a Singapore clinic may send

**Target:** PDPA WhatsApp marketing Singapore
**Why:** the question that stalls clinic deals, and nobody has written
it plainly for clinic owners rather than for lawyers.
**Outline:** what counts as marketing vs a service message · consent
at point of collection and what a compliant form actually says ·
withdrawal handling · retention periods · what a DPA with a vendor
covers · a checklist a clinic manager can run against their own setup.
**Cite:** PDPA 2012; PDPC advisory guidelines on consent and
notification. **Links to:** `/compliance`, `/aesthetic-clinics`.

### 2. The Do Not Call registry and patient reactivation campaigns

**Target:** Do Not Call registry Singapore business rules
**Outline:** what Part 9 covers and that it reaches app messages to a
phone number · why "existing business relationship" is narrower than
most assume · screening numbers before a campaign · the difference
between a recall reminder and an offer · what to record and keep.
**Cite:** PDPA Part 9; PDPC DNC guidance. **Links to:** `/compliance`,
`/solutions`, post 1.

### 3. MOH advertising rules and what your chatbot is allowed to say

**Target:** MOH advertising rules aesthetic clinic
**Outline:** HCSA advertising provisions in plain English · outcome
claims, comparisons, testimonials and inducements · why a general
chatbot improvising about treatments is a licensing risk and not just
a brand one · how approved-wording templates work · where the human
handover must sit. **Cite:** Healthcare Services Act 2020; MOH
advertising guidance. **Links to:** `/compliance`, `/aesthetic-clinics`.

### 4. How to reduce consultation no-shows at a Singapore clinic

**Target:** reduce no-shows clinic Singapore
**Outline:** the reasons people miss consultations, separated into the
ones a reminder fixes and the ones it does not · reminder timing and
why more is not better · deposits and PayNow, and the goodwill cost ·
making rescheduling one tap · what to measure.
**Honesty note:** an existing published ArkFlow article already cites
research finding automated reminders **less** effective than staff
calls for some cohorts. Do not contradict it. **Links to:**
`/solutions`, `/how-it-works`.

### 5. WhatsApp Business app vs WhatsApp Business API: which a clinic needs

**Target:** WhatsApp Business API Singapore
**Outline:** the two products and why the names confuse everyone ·
device limits, multiple staff, one number · template approval and the
24-hour window · what the API costs and who the BSP is · when the free
app is genuinely the right answer, which is more often than vendors
admit. **Cite:** Meta's own product and pricing documentation.
**Links to:** `/what-we-build`, `/compliance`.

### 6. How fast should a Singapore clinic reply to an enquiry?
